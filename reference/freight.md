# Annual US new freight cars

Annual US new freight cars, 1947-1993. Freight cars, new (excl.
rebuilt), new orders, equip. mfrers. Series N0193 from the M3
competition.

## Usage

``` r
freight
```

## Format

Annual time series of length 47.

## Source

Hyndman, R.J., Koehler, A.B., Ord, J.K., and Snyder, R.D. (2008)
*Forecasting with exponential smoothing: the state space approach*,
Springer.
[doi:10.1007/978-3-540-71918-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-71918-2)
.
